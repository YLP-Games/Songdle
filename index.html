🎵 Songdle

How to play

  1. Press the big ▶ play button to hear a tiny piece of a song.
  2. Start typing the song's name and tap it in the list.
  3. Not sure? Press Skip to hear a longer piece. Each wrong guess also makes it longer. You get 5 tries.
  4. The coloured boxes help you:
    Green = right. Orange = one of the singers is right. Red = wrong.
    For the year, an arrow ▲ means the song is newer, ▼ means it is older.
  5. Today's song is the same for everyone, so you can compare scores.
