How to play

  1. Press the big green ▶ play button to hear a tiny piece of a song.
  2. Start typing the song's name and tap it in the list.
  3. Not sure? Press Skip to hear a longer piece. Each wrong guess also makes it longer. You get 5 tries.
  4. The coloured boxes help you:
    Green = right. Orange = one of the singers is right. Red = wrong.
    For the year, ▲ means the song is newer, ▼ means it is older.
  5. Today's song is the same for everyone, so you can compare scores.

Add your own songs

In Spotify, Apple Music or YouTube Music, open a playlist or album, tap ••• or ⋮ → Share → Copy link, then paste it here.

Spotify brings in up to 100 songs, YouTube Music up to 200. Added lists are saved on this phone or computer only.
